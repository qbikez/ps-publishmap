How one scenario samples

Measure-Scenario in configmap/test/perf/Invoke-PerfSuite.ps1. This loop runs once per scenario, per side, per round: 3 rounds × base + head = 6 times per job, each time in a fresh pwsh.

while ($times.Count -lt $Iterations -and
       ($times.Count -lt 3 -or $budget.Elapsed.TotalSeconds -lt $ScenarioBudgetSeconds)) { ... }
# stops at 30 samples, or at 10 s once it has at least 3, whichever comes first
1 · Warm-upRuns the action once, untimed. It runs before the budget clock starts and is not a sample.
2 · Timed loopSetup (e.g. touch a map file) → read allocations → stopwatch around the action. Every sample is kept.
3 · Stop30 samples, or at least 3 samples and 10 s elapsed. The budget is wall-clock, so Setup and the allocation reads count against it.
4 · ReportMedian, p95, min and median allocated KB. Compare-Perf takes the median of the 3 per-round medians.
  • Minimum 3 samples. A 5 s call still runs 3 times (~15 s) before the budget can stop it.
  • Budget wins above ~333 ms per call (10 s ÷ 30). In the CI fixtures only the list scenarios are that slow.
  • new-shell is different. It skips this loop and always starts 1 untimed + NewShellIterations (5) fresh pwsh processes, with no budget.
